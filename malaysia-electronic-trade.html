Product and compatibility

Companion Clock is an offline animal companion and clock for iPhone with iOS 15 or later.

The raccoon and Rain by the window are free.

Golden retriever: a separate one-time purchase that permanently unlocks this animal, all paid sounds and the sound mixer.

Groundhog: a separate one-time purchase that permanently unlocks this animal, all paid sounds and the sound mixer.

Buying one animal does not unlock the other paid animal; either purchase unlocks the same paid sounds and mixing of up to three sounds.

There is no subscription, recurring charge, advertising or app account, and the app collects no personal data.
